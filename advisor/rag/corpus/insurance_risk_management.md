# Insurance and Protecting Your Finances

## Term vs. whole life insurance
Term life insurance covers a fixed period, such as 20 or 30 years, and is much cheaper per dollar of coverage. Whole life insurance combines lifelong coverage with a cash-value component and carries substantially higher premiums. For most people whose goal is replacing income for dependents, term coverage is considered sufficient; a common rough guide is coverage of 10-12 times annual income.

## Disability insurance
Disability insurance replaces part of income if illness or injury prevents working. For working-age adults, the probability of a long-term disability is meaningful, and income is often their largest financial asset.

## Umbrella and other coverage
Umbrella liability insurance adds coverage above auto and home policies at relatively low cost. Health insurance protects against catastrophic medical bills, which are a leading contributor to financial hardship.
