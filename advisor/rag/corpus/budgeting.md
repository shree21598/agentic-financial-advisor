# Budgeting Basics

## The 50/30/20 rule
The 50/30/20 rule, popularized by Elizabeth Warren and Amelia Warren Tyagi, splits after-tax income into 50% for needs (housing, utilities, groceries, insurance, minimum debt payments), 30% for wants (dining out, entertainment, travel), and 20% for savings and extra debt payments. It is a simple starting framework; people in high-cost areas may need to adjust the proportions.

## Pay yourself first
"Pay yourself first" means automatically moving savings out of each paycheck before discretionary spending, rather than saving whatever is left at month end.

## Housing costs
A common guideline is to keep total housing costs around 28-30% of gross income or less. Lenders often look at the debt-to-income ratio, the share of gross monthly income going to debt payments.

## Tracking spending
Reviewing a few months of bank and card statements reveals where money actually goes. Small recurring charges, such as unused subscriptions, are common sources of savings.
